## Private and unpublished references

Some maintainers may use private companion projects while preparing community
material. They are intentionally not linked here: a public guide must not turn
an authenticated or private repository into an installation promise. Add a
companion project only after its owner has confirmed its public URL, license,
publication state, prerequisites, and last-reviewed date.

## Curation policy

A future community entry should include:

- repository URL and owner;
- what it does;
- license/publication state;
- last reviewed date;
- installation risk or prerequisites;
- whether it is official, community, or reference-only.

Do not copy third-party code, documentation or private payloads into this repository.
